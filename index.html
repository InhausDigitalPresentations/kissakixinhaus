Where it comes from

In New York, this place is never quiet.

Serious sushi, not so serious room. Music up, cocktails flying, chefs who are part of the party. That's Kissaki on the Bowery, any night of the week.

The brand

Not the quiet side of Japanese dining.

Let's be honest, Abu Dhabi doesn't need another Japanese restaurant. It has plenty, and most of them whisper.

The problem

Good sushi is everywhere.

So "we're Japanese" is not a reason to come.

The difference

Same craft, more noise.

Great food, great music, great cocktails, great room, great crowd. All at the same time.

The position

Japanese craft. New York energy. Now in Abu Dhabi.

What Carbone did for Italian in Dubai. The attitude lands together with the menu.

The idea

The night moves east.

We're not opening a restaurant. We're moving a night from New York to Abu Dhabi, and everybody gets to watch.

01 Pre-opening · Three weeks out

The launch film.

Two chefs, a set of knives and a yellow cab. No voiceover, no faces, and we never show the new restaurant. Yes, on purpose.

Format60" film + cut-downs
LocationsNew York · Abu Dhabi
FeelClose, handheld, loud
1 Int. Kissaki, the Bowery · Night

We start in the middle of it.

Friday night, full volume. No intro. We're already inside and already late.

SoundJust the room. No music yet.
2 The counter · Continuous

Two chefs pack their knives.

Right in the middle of service. They roll them up, buckle the leather and nobody even looks. Weird? A bit. That's the hook.

SoundSteel, leather, buckle. Then the track kicks in.
3 Ext. the Bowery · Night

Arm up. Whistle.

The most New York move ever invented. A yellow cab pulls over.

SoundThe whistle stops the music for half a second.
4 Int. yellow cab · Moving

The knives ride in the back.

Door shuts. New York goes by the window. We cut on the slam.

Cut.

11,000 km later
5 Ext. Saadiyat, Abu Dhabi · Night

Same door. Different city.

Palm trees, warm air and a New York cab that has no business being here. That's the screenshot.

SoundThe city changes. The beat doesn't.
6 Kissaki Abu Dhabi · The door

They go in. We don't.

The door closes. Black. Then you hear the room wake up. We never show the night. You want to see it, you book it.

02 Opening · The first three months

Full from night one.

For three months this has to be the busiest room in town. PR fills it with the right people. We're there with the cameras. Simple deal.

Night one

One night out in New York.

Flash photos at the door, a New York DJ, the tuna cut in front of everyone. Then the lights go down and it becomes Kissaki After Dark.

Nights 2 to 90

We shoot the room, not the guest list.

A famous face is one story. A packed room is a reputation. So we film the toast, the knife, the bar three deep. Tagging people is PR's job.

Extra idea · If we can get one

What if the cab was real?

A New York yellow cab driving around Abu Dhabi before we open. No logo, just NYC — AUH on the door. People will do the rest.

We can't promise the car. If it happens, great. If not, the plan doesn't need it.
