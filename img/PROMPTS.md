# Kissaki deck — image prompts

Generate each one, save it in this folder under the exact filename, and the site picks it up on reload. Missing files are fine: the slide falls back to the light-streak background.

Attach 2–3 real Kissaki Bowery photos (from their site or Instagram) to every NYC prompt as reference, so the room is recognisable.

## Style block (paste at the end of every prompt)

> Shot on 35mm, direct on-camera flash mixed with slow shutter (1/8s), heavy motion blur and light trails, handheld, slightly off-level, deep blacks, warm tungsten highlights, visible film grain. No faces in focus: backs, hands, shoulders, blur. Crowded, loud, alive. No text, no logos, no watermarks. Not posed, not stock.

Backgrounds are 16:9. Storyboard frames are 4:5 vertical.
